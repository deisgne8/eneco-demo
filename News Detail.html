Planning a clearer
product enquiry.

The product information that helps structure a useful B2B supply conversation.

A well-prepared enquiry helps create a more efficient supply conversation. Here's what to consider before reaching out to a product supplier.

Every successful supply arrangement begins with a clear understanding of the requirement. Whether sourcing petroleum products, fertilizers, petrochemicals or industrial fluids, providing the right information at the enquiry stage helps establish expectations and coordinate the next steps more effectively.

A product enquiry is more than a request for pricing. It is an opportunity to communicate technical requirements, understand supply possibilities and establish a practical route towards delivery.

01. Start with the right product information

Begin by identifying the product you require. Include the product name, preferred grade and any relevant technical specifications.

Different grades and product variations can have different properties, applications and supply requirements. Being specific from the beginning helps avoid unnecessary clarification and ensures that the discussion starts with the right product.

Consider including:

  • Product name and grade
  • Required specifications or standards
  • Intended application
  • Any preferred product alternatives

02. Define your quantity requirements

The required quantity plays an important role in understanding the scope of a supply enquiry.

Indicate the estimated volume, whether the requirement is a one-time purchase or part of a recurring supply arrangement, and any expected changes in demand.

Providing a realistic quantity range helps establish a clearer basis for discussing availability, commercial arrangements and delivery coordination.

03. Consider packaging and transportation

Product requirements extend beyond the material itself. Packaging preferences and transportation arrangements can influence how a supply requirement is coordinated.

Depending on the product, consider whether bulk, drums, bags, containers or other suitable packaging formats are required.

It is also useful to identify the destination, preferred transportation method and any specific handling or delivery considerations.

Key details to communicate:

  • Preferred packaging format
  • Delivery destination
  • Required delivery timeframe
  • Transportation or handling preferences

04. Share your expected timeline

A clear delivery timeline helps align the supply conversation with your procurement requirements.

Indicate whether the requirement is immediate, planned for a future date or part of an ongoing procurement schedule. If there are specific delivery windows or project deadlines, communicate these at the enquiry stage.

Early clarity around timing supports better coordination between sourcing, commercial arrangements and logistics planning.

05. Keep technical documentation in mind

Technical documentation provides important information for evaluating product suitability and meeting procurement requirements.

Depending on the product and application, relevant documents may include Technical Data Sheets (TDS), Safety Data Sheets (SDS), Certificates of Analysis (COA) and quality-related documentation.

If particular certifications, specifications or supporting documents are required, mention them when submitting your enquiry.

Bringing the details together

A clear product enquiry does not need to be complicated. It simply needs to communicate the essential information that helps move the supply conversation forward.

By defining the product, grade, quantity, destination, packaging and expected timeline, buyers can establish a more structured starting point for discussing sourcing possibilities and delivery arrangements.
